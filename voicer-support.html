Voicer Support

Hold Fn, speak, then release to paste your words at the cursor. Speech recognition runs on your Mac.

First setup

  1. Launch Voicer and allow Microphone and Accessibility in System Settings.
  2. In System Settings > Keyboard, set “Press Globe key to” to “Do Nothing”.
  3. Let the Whisper large-v3-turbo model finish its one-time download from Hugging Face, about 1.6 GB.
  4. Open a text field, hold Fn, speak and release. Double-tap Fn for hands-free recording, then press Fn to finish. Esc cancels.

Voicer requires macOS 14 or later and an Apple Silicon Mac. It lives in the menu bar. There is no account, subscription or in-app purchase.

Choose your language

Open the Voicer menu > Settings > General. Auto uses your Mac's language if Whisper supports it, otherwise it detects the language from speech. You can explicitly select English, Russian, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish and other supported languages. Accuracy varies with language, microphone and recording conditions.

Fn or pasting does not work

Check System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility and Microphone. Make sure the App Store copy of Voicer is enabled. If the setup window asks for Input Monitoring, enable it too. Restart Voicer if macOS asks you to. In passive monitoring mode, Esc also reaches the active app.

Try an ordinary TextEdit document first. Some secure fields and applications restrict synthetic paste. You can always copy a transcript from History. Avoid running both editions with the Fn shortcut at the same time.

The model download failed

Check your internet connection and free disk space. Choose the model status in Voicer's menu, then Download model to retry. Keep the Mac awake during the download. Recognition works offline after setup.

Personal dictionary

Open Settings > Dictionary. Enter one term per line, for example Voicer = voycer, then Save. The App Store edition uses only this manual dictionary. It does not build a dictionary from other files on your Mac.

Optional local corrections

Russian dictation can use your own LM Studio server at localhost:1234. Start the server, load a model in LM Studio, enable corrections in Voicer and enter its model identifier. Voicer never loads or unloads that model for you. If it is unavailable, recognition continues without corrections. Other languages skip the LLM and Russian speech cleanup.

History and privacy

The last 50 transcripts and 20 audio clips stay on this Mac. Use History to copy a transcript or clear the transcript list. Read the privacy policy for storage and removal details.

Get help

Write to hegona3@gmail.com. Include the Voicer version from About, your macOS version and what happened. Share a transcript or recording only if you want to and it contains no private information.
