Mac App Store edition

Voicer Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

We do not collect your data

The Mac App Store edition of Voicer has no analytics, advertising, tracking, developer telemetry or account system. It does not send audio, transcripts, dictionary entries or dictation history to the developer.

Speech stays on your Mac

Voicer records while you dictate and runs Whisper large-v3-turbo on your Mac. Recognized text is placed on the system clipboard to paste into the active text field. Clipboard restoration is enabled by default. The app you paste into handles that text under its own privacy policy.

Local storage

Your settings, personal dictionary, the latest 50 dictations and the latest 20 audio clips are stored in Voicer's sandbox container on your Mac. They are not synced by Voicer. Local diagnostic logs may contain short transcript excerpts when recognition or optional corrections fail. The App Store edition does not scan your project folders, shell history or Claude Code history.

You can clear the transcript list from History. To remove all local files, including saved clips and the downloaded model, quit Voicer and remove its container at ~/Library/Containers/com.empq.voicer.appstore in Finder. Removing only the app may leave its container behind.

One-time model download

On first launch, Voicer downloads the Whisper model from Hugging Face over HTTPS. Hugging Face and its download infrastructure receive the network information needed to deliver the file, such as your IP address. No dictation audio or text is sent with this download. See Hugging Face's privacy policy. After the model is downloaded, speech recognition works offline.

Optional LM Studio corrections

Corrections are off by default and available for Russian dictation. If enabled, Voicer sends recognized text and your term list only to LM Studio running on this same Mac at localhost:1234. You must start the local server and load the model yourself. Voicer does not use a cloud LLM endpoint.

Permissions

Microphone permission is used for recording. Accessibility event-posting permission allows the Fn gesture and pasting with Cmd+V. If an active event tap is unavailable, Input Monitoring is used to detect Fn with a passive listener. Voicer does not store other keystrokes or use Accessibility to read your documents.

Apple and TestFlight

Apple may provide crash reports and TestFlight feedback according to your device settings and Apple's terms. Voicer does not include a separate crash-reporting service. See Apple's privacy policy.

Contact

This policy applies specifically to the sandboxed Mac App Store and TestFlight edition with bundle ID com.empq.voicer.appstore. The separately distributed full edition has different features and diagnostics.
