Nightrail — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Nightrail is a single-player mobile game. There is no sign-in, no account and no purchases. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera data.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No data is sold or shared with data brokers.

Game progress

Your progress (train, crew, resources, settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. We cannot read or restore it.

Anonymous diagnostics

During the beta the app sends anonymous technical events so we can find crashes and broken screens: a random install identifier generated on the device, app version, device model and OS version, and which screens or actions were used. These events contain no personal data and cannot be linked to you as a person. They are stored on Cloudflare infrastructure operated by the developer and are used only to improve the game.

TestFlight

While the app is distributed through TestFlight, Apple collects its own crash and usage data under Apple's own terms. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from them.

Your choices

To stop all diagnostics, delete the app. Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing tied to you to export or erase; if you have a question, write to us and we will answer.
