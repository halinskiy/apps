Quirky Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

We do not collect your data

The Mac App Store edition of Quirky has no analytics, advertising, tracking, developer telemetry or account system. It does not send screenshots, recognized text, colors or measurements to the developer or to anyone else.

Everything happens on your Mac

When you press the capture shortcut, Quirky takes a snapshot of your screen and keeps it in memory only while the capture overlay is open. Text recognition uses Apple's Vision framework on your Mac. The result, such as text, a color code or a size in pixels, is placed on the system clipboard. The snapshot is discarded when the overlay closes and is never written to disk.

Local storage

Quirky stores only its settings, such as enabled modes, the last used mode and the highlight color, in its sandbox container at ~/Library/Containers/com.threempq.quirky. To remove them, quit Quirky and delete that folder.

Permissions

Screen Recording permission is required to capture the screen for text recognition, color picking and pixel measurement. Quirky uses it only while you are capturing.

Apple

Apple may provide crash reports according to your device settings and Apple's terms. Quirky does not include a separate crash-reporting service. See Apple's privacy policy.

Contact

Kostiantyn Halynskyi: hegona3@gmail.com. If you send a support email, the information you choose to include is used only to answer it.

This policy applies to the sandboxed Mac App Store edition with bundle ID com.threempq.quirky. The separately distributed edition adds browser modes that talk to your browser over Apple Events.
