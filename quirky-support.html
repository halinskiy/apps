Quirky Support

Press Cmd+Shift+1 anywhere to freeze the screen and capture. The result lands on your clipboard.

First setup

  1. Launch Quirky. It lives in the menu bar, not the Dock.
  2. Allow Screen Recording in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording, then quit and reopen Quirky.
  3. Press Cmd+Shift+1 to start your first capture.

Quirky requires macOS 13 or later. There is no account, subscription or in-app purchase.

Modes

  • OCR copies text. Drag over a region or click a single word.
  • HEX copies the color code of the pixel under the cursor.
  • SPX measures distances in pixels. Rulers stop at the edges of elements on screen. Press H or V to keep a horizontal or vertical measurement, hold Shift to measure across the whole screen.

Shortcuts

  • Cmd+Shift+1 starts a capture in the mode you used last.
  • Tab and Shift+Tab switch to the next or previous mode during a capture.
  • Esc closes the capture at any time.
  • Click the menu bar icon to pick a mode. Right-click it for settings: which modes are enabled, highlight color and Quit.

The capture shows only the wallpaper or a black screen

Screen Recording permission is missing or out of date. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording, turn Quirky off and on again, then quit and reopen it.

The shortcut does nothing

Another app may already use Cmd+Shift+1. Quit that app or change its shortcut, then try again. You can always start a capture by clicking a mode in the menu bar popover.
